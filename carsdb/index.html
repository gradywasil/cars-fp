05 / The one that isn’t about a film

1,455 little cars. One list.

Somewhere along the line, loving these films turned into a spreadsheet problem. CarsDB is the answer to it — a searchable catalogue of every Disney and Pixar Cars die-cast we could get our hands on, from Lightning McQueen to a blimp.

03 / How it worksThree things, done properly

Browse.
Rate.
Keep track.

No storefront, no price guide, no valuations. CarsDB is a reference and a checklist — the two things that are genuinely hard to find when the line runs to well over a thousand vehicles.

01

Browse

Start typing and the catalogue narrows as you go. Open the filter panel to cut it down by series or tag — or several at once, when you are trying to remember which Mater short the one with the ghost came from.

  • Search names, series & tags
  • Sort A–Z, Z–A, or by rating
  • Filter by 30 series, 17 tags
02

Rate

Five stars, half-steps allowed, one vote each. It is not a market value and it is not a rarity score. It is a room full of people quietly agreeing that some sculpts are better than others.

  • Half-star precision
  • Ratings shown with a vote count
  • Sort the whole catalogue by it
03

Keep track

Two lists. One for what is already on the shelf, one for what is not yet. Then filter the catalogue down to either — which is how you avoid buying the same Doc Hudson twice.

  • My owned & my wishlist
  • Filter the catalogue to either list
  • Tied to a free account, not a browser

Go find
your shelf.

Browsing needs nothing at all. Rating and tracking need a free account, and that is the whole extent of the paperwork.

The fine print / what this page is

A front door, not the app. This page introduces CarsDB, an independent catalogue built by Arranged Godly. Everything interactive — the search, the ratings, the owned and wishlist tracking — lives on that site behind a free account. This page holds no catalogue data of its own.

The numbers. The counts on this page (1,455 vehicles, 30 series, 17 tags, 61 pages of results) and the six example ratings were read directly from the live catalogue in September 2026. They change as the catalogue grows and as people vote, so treat them as a snapshot rather than a running total.

The pictures. Every illustration here is original stylised fan art. None of it is a photograph of a Mattel product, and none of it identifies a specific release, paint scheme, or product code. CarsDB is not a price guide, a rarity index, an appraisal service, or a shop — it does not publish values and nothing is for sale.

Nothing to save. This page has no forms, no downloads, no clipboard access, and no local storage of its own. The only thing the surrounding club site remembers is which of its five pages you have opened.
