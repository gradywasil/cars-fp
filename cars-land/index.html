01 / THE CASEEXHIBITS A–D

NOT JUST
A LAND.
A feeling.

Other lands ask you to believe in another world. Cars Land makes you wonder why you ever left this one.

✳

A thoroughly unscientific argument.
Supported by some very real magic.

A desert skyline. A small-town heartbeat. A finish line worth chasing. This is our case for Cars Land as the definitive pinnacle of the happiest place on Earth.

AThe world is the attraction.+

Twelve acres recreate the town of Radiator Springs, with automobile-inspired rock formations on the horizon. [1] Our argument: the scenery isn’t a backdrop. It’s the reason to slow down.

THE IMMERSION ARGUMENT
BThe headliner has range.+

Radiator Springs Racers pairs a scenic story-driven ride with a side-by-side race. [2] It has the nerve to be lovely before it gets loud. We call that emotional horsepower.

THE ONE-MORE-RIDE ARGUMENT
CThe small stuff is the big stuff.+

A roadside-inspired diner. Snack stands shaped like traffic cones. Even the landscaping gets automotive details. [1] Here, a snack break doesn’t interrupt the story. It’s another scene.

THE NOTHING-IS-JUST-DECOR ARGUMENT
DThen the lights come on.+

At sunset, Radiator Springs’ neon lights bring the town into its nighttime chapter. [4] The daytime case is already strong. The nighttime case is, frankly, showing off.

04 / THE VERDICTDELIBERATION COMPLETE

CASE.
CLOSED.

Some lands have a great ride.
Cars Land is a great feeling.

It’s the joy of going somewhere new and somehow feeling like you’ve come home. The thrill, the stillness, the neon, the ridiculous little cones. Not one perfect moment. A whole town of them.

The definitive pinnacle of the happiest place on Earth.
That’s our story. We’re sticking to it.
