Cross-examination

Objection?
Overruled.*

*Respectfully. Reasonable people can disagree. The bureau simply enjoys making a very good case.

“But it’s barely a racing movie.”+
Exactly: it’s a spy movie that arrived wearing a racing-movie name tag. Judged as a genre switch rather than a rerun, its weirdness becomes the attraction. That won’t work for everyone. It works for this bureau.
“There’s too much Mater.”+
We submit that his lack of polish is the emotional argument. Coolness is optional. Loyalty is not. You may still find the jokes grating; the bureau cannot subpoena your sense of humor.
“What about the reviews?”+
A critical consensus is worth reading. It is not a set of instructions for how you are allowed to feel. The defense is not claiming unanimous acclaim; it is making a case for a film whose particular brand of weirdness deserves another look. Inspect the opposing case ↗
“You can’t objectively prove a masterpiece.”+
Correct. Art isn’t a theorem. This is a fan’s argument, not a scientific finding or a fabricated critical consensus. The film details are sourced. The conclusions are subjective. The enthusiasm is, regrettably, incurable.

I need everyone in this thread to understand that Cars 2 is not “underrated.” Cars 2 is the greatest film ever committed to any medium.

In 2011 Pixar had a billion-dollar franchise about a race car learning humility in a small town, looked directly at it, and said: what if the tow truck was a spy. Nobody stopped them. Tokyo. Porto Corsa. Paris. London. A global conspiracy run by lemons. Michael Caine. MICHAEL CAINE.

They strapped a character into a chair and killed him on screen with a giant camera lens, in a movie that also sells toothbrushes.

You call it the worst Pixar film because you sat down expecting Cars again and got a Cold War thriller starring the comic relief, and you were not emotionally equipped. Mater is not the flaw. Mater is the thesis. It is a film about a guy everyone treats like an idiot turning out to be right about absolutely everything, and the audience responded by treating the film like an idiot. Do you see it. DO YOU SEE IT.

Cars 3 is a good movie about getting older. Cars 2 is what happens when a studio gets to do whatever it wants and nobody in the room is a coward.

It is not a guilty pleasure. There is no guilt. Ka-chow.

Written by fans, for pasting. Deploy in group chats, quote-tweets, and any comment section that has it coming. Every factual detail above is sourced further down the page; every judgment is ours and is, we admit, extremely loud.

The jury is you.

So.
Masterpiece?

A sports sequel became a globe-trotting spy comedy and committed completely. The ambition is enormous. The world is gorgeous. The heart is a rusty tow truck.
We call that cinema.
